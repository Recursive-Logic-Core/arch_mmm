## 2. Experimental Constraints & Baseline

* **Zero-Preparation Blind Run:** The candidate had never previously encountered, prepared for, or practiced this standardized assessment battery. All problem sets were ingested and resolved live without pre-cached answers or templates.
* **Physical & Architectural Decoupling (Air-Gap Setup):**
  * The AI model had **zero execution access** to the candidate's testing workstation.
  * No browser extensions, automated scripts, or API integrations were running locally.
  * Zero tab-switching or focus-loss flags were triggered during the proctored sessions.
* **Human-in-the-Loop Routing:**
  * The human architect operated as the sensory I/O node, feeding raw visual/text context to the external reasoning engine via an out-of-band channel.
  * The AI processed logical decomposition, edge-case evaluation, and syntax validation.
  * Final decision execution was confirmed manually by the operator under tight per-question latency budgets.

---

## 3. Structural Insights & Latency Dynamics

* **Logic & Cloud Acceleration:** In high-abstraction domains (*Software Architecture* and *Microsoft Azure*), execution times were cut significantly below the time cap (e.g., Software Architecture solved in under 45% of allocated time) while maintaining a 98th+ percentile accuracy score.
* **Linguistic & Audio Latency Overhead:** The English B2 module exhibited the highest time consumption (`11:54 / 12:00 min`) due to multimodal conversion overhead (audio-to-text capture latencies and extensive passage ingestion). Even with one unanswered terminal item, baseline precision secured a 96th percentile outcome.

---

## 4. Conclusion & Operational Viability

This case study demonstrates that **symbiotic Human-AI orchestration** outperforms isolated human recall and unconstrained brute-force querying. High-density context framing, combined with disciplined human-supervised execution, delivers deterministic, top-percentile problem solving across diverse engineering stacks in real-time environments.
